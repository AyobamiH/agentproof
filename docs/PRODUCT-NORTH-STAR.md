# Product north star

> An agent can perform an authorised action and produce independently verifiable evidence of exactly what happened, under whose authority, with exactly-once execution and deterministic recovery.

AgentProof serves coding agents and autonomous operators through a portable CLI, SDK, schemas, and receipts. Developers are the adoption path. Agent platforms, engineering teams, and enterprises are the buyers of shared policy, production authority, retained evidence, incident recovery, and fleet controls.

The pain is not access to another capable model. It is proving that a consequential action was the intended action, was properly authorised, happened once, produced the required target state, and can be recovered or escalated honestly. The commercial value is fewer bad changes and false-success claims, safer retries, less manual review, faster recovery, and portable audit evidence.

AgentProof differentiates through transaction-bound authority, independent postcondition verification, explicit compensation semantics, deterministic recovery, and offline-verifiable signed evidence across runtimes. It does not compete as another agent framework, directory, or observability dashboard.

Adoption begins with a small open portable package on the action path. Repeat use and measured outcomes justify production authority and paid team/enterprise controls. Framework neutrality is mandatory: integrations adapt to AgentProof’s public contracts. OpenClaw remains the first integration and dogfood environment, not the permanent source boundary.
